Start of a New Month

Everything you do in the scheduler app from the moment a new month opens until the physicians' requests are in ByteBloc. Follow the stages in order.

The buttons you will use live along the top of the app. They look like this, and this page uses the same pictures whenever it tells you to click one:

Send Monthly Shift Requests Physician Roster Scheduling Rules Settings

Hovering the mouse over any button in the app shows its name, so if a picture here doesn't match what you see, hover and read the name instead.

Before you start Two things to have ready

  • This month's request template. The Excel file physicians fill in, the same "Master Preferences" workbook that has always gone out, already set up for the new month with the right dates along the top. Save it somewhere easy to find, for example a folder called KEA Scheduling with the month in the file name, such as February 2027 Master Preferences.xlsx.
  • The app open and pointed at the right computer. Click Settings and check the Backend choice. Local means the scheduler runs on this computer, which is what you want for the steps on this page. Remote is only for long schedule-building runs on the server.

1 Check the roster

Goal: the list of physicians the app will email and schedule is correct for this month.

  1. Click Physician Roster. A window opens with every physician down the left side; click a name to see their details on the right. There is a search box at the top of the list.
  2. For anyone who has left the department, untick Active. Don't remove them; inactive physicians are kept but not emailed or scheduled.
  3. For anyone who is new, use the add button at the top of the list, fill in first and last name, and make sure Active is ticked.
  4. Check each person's Email. This is the address the request link goes to, so a typo means they never receive it.
  5. Tick Casual for casual staff. Casuals are only given shifts after everyone else has their requested number.
  6. If a physician has an admin role and shouldn't be given early or night shifts they didn't ask for, tick Admin role, exempt from mandatory anchors.
  7. Click Save after each person you change. A small "Saved." appears next to the button.

The Scheduling Rules button shows, in plain English, every rule the scheduler applies to each physician. Useful if someone asks why they keep getting a certain pattern. It is read-only.

2 Send the monthly requests

Goal: every active physician receives an email with their personal link to fill in their preferences online.

  1. Click Send Monthly Shift Requests. A window opens.
  2. Leave Load existing period on "new period". The app pre-fills the next month for you: the Period ID (for example 2027-02), the Label physicians will see (for example February 2027), and the dates the submission window opens and closes. Check the month and year are the one you mean. The close date is the deadline physicians see; the 15th is the usual default and you can change it.
  3. Next to Master schedule template (.xlsx), click the browse button and pick the template file you prepared. This is the file physicians will fill in online.
  4. If you want a note in the email, type it under Additional message. For example: "Please block your holiday requests before submitting."
  5. Click Send Monthly Shift Requests at the bottom. The app asks you to confirm, then sends one email to every active physician who has an email on file. This takes a minute or two; leave the window open until it finishes.

If someone says they didn't get the email

Open the same window, choose the month under Load existing period, and a list of physicians appears below the form. Find the person and click the send-again button beside their name. Only that person is emailed. First check their address in the roster, since a wrong address is the usual cause.

If the window says sked or email is "not configured", the app can't send anything from this computer. That is a setup problem on the computer, not a mistake in your steps; it needs the configuration files copied over.

3 Collect the requests as they come in

Goal: pull everyone's answers into the app and see who still hasn't submitted.

  1. On the main screen, in the Schedule Setup box, click From Web (sked).
  2. Under Sked Period, pick the month you sent out. Check the Month and Year fields just below it say the same month. If they disagree, every date in the import will be wrong.
  3. Click Pull In Requests. The app downloads every submitted request and checks each one against the rules.
  4. A list titled Not submitted yet shows anyone who hasn't finished. "Draft" means they started but didn't press submit; "Not started" means they never opened the link. As the deadline gets close, click Send reminder beside a name to email that person a reminder with their link.

You can pull in requests as many times as you like. Each pull replaces the previous one with whatever has been submitted so far, so do a final pull after the deadline.

4 Review and fix what was submitted

Goal: every physician's request is marked Valid, or you have decided to accept its problems.

After a pull, the Validate table lists every physician with their requested number of shifts and a green Valid or red Invalid badge. Click a row to see the specific problems, for example "fewer than the minimum available days" or "no night shifts offered".

  1. Look at the request itself. Beside each name is a small file icon. Clicking it opens that physician's filled-in spreadsheet in Excel, so you can see exactly what they marked. Only works when the app is set to the Local backend.
  2. Tell people what to fix. Click Generate Error Report. A window lists every physician with problems and what each problem is. Beside each person is Send Reminder Email, which emails that physician their own list of problems and their link so they can correct and resubmit. Pull in requests again once they have.
  3. Accept problems you're fine with. Open a physician's row and click Override next to a problem to accept it, or Override All Errors to accept all of theirs at once. An overridden problem is greyed out and no longer counts against them. Physicians marked "Special Provisions" in the roster are overridden automatically.

Aim for zero red badges before the next step, either by people resubmitting or by you overriding. The count of valid physicians is shown at the top of the table.

5 Send the availability to ByteBloc

Goal: ByteBloc has each physician's availability, so scheduling can continue there as before.

  1. Click Send Requests to ByteBloc at the top of the Validate table. A panel opens showing a preview: how many requests will be sent and for whom. Nothing has been sent yet.
  2. Leave Only send changes ticked unless you have a reason not to. It sends only what is new or different since the last time, which is what you normally want.
  3. Read the preview. If something looks wrong, close the panel and fix it in the steps above.
  4. Type the word CONFIRM in capitals in the box. This is deliberate: the button stays disabled until the word is typed exactly.
  5. Click Send to ByteBloc and wait for "Sent successfully".

ByteBloc accepts one send every 15 minutes. If you click send twice in a row, the second one is refused. Make your corrections, then send once. If you do need a second send, wait 15 minutes first.

From here, carry on in ByteBloc as you normally would. Building the schedule inside this app is the next phase, and it has its own instructions.
